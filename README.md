# Customer Behavior Analysis

## Overview

Customer Behavior Analysis is an end-to-end data analytics project focused on extracting actionable business insights from customer transaction data. The project explores purchasing patterns, customer segmentation, revenue trends, discount effectiveness, and product performance using SQL, Python (EDA), and Power BI.

---

## Objectives

* Analyze customer purchase behavior and trends
* Segment customers into New, Returning, and Loyal categories
* Evaluate revenue distribution across segments
* Measure the impact of discounts on purchasing behavior
* Identify top-performing products based on ratings and revenue
* Build business-ready KPIs and dashboard metrics

---

## Tools & Technologies

* **PostgreSQL** – Data storage and querying
* **SQL** – Aggregations, segmentation, KPI calculations
* **Python (Pandas, Matplotlib/Seaborn)** – Exploratory Data Analysis
* **Power BI** – Interactive dashboard and visualization

---

## Key Analyses Performed

* Revenue analysis by age group and customer segment
* Top 5 products by average rating
* Discount percentage analysis by product
* Customer segmentation based on previous purchases
* Purchase frequency and behavioral trend evaluation

---

## Project Workflow

1. Data import and cleaning
2. Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
3. SQL-based metric calculation
4. Customer segmentation logic implementation
5. Visualization and dashboard creation

---

## Insights Generated

* Identification of high-value customer segments
* Revenue contribution breakdown by demographic groups
* Effectiveness of discount strategies
* Product performance ranking

---

## Purpose

This project demonstrates practical business intelligence skills, including SQL aggregation logic, segmentation modeling, KPI design, and dashboard-ready analytics suitable for real-world business scenarios.
